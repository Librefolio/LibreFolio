# F-100 — Yield on Cost (YOC)

## What it does
A YOC column in the Dashboard's positions (Holdings) table: the last year's gross dividends and interest per unit
held, against the position's average cost per unit. A hover cell explains the value or, when it cannot be computed,
why (missing FX, missing cost, a payout with no eligible quantity, too little history…).

## Layer breakdown
**Backend**: `backend/app/services/yield_on_cost.py` — `calculate_yield_on_cost_for_positions()` per
`(asset, broker)`, called by `PortfolioService` when it builds holdings; the result travels as
`PortfolioHolding.yield_on_cost` (`YieldOnCostResult`: status, value, reason, provenance with the income count, the
per-unit income and the FX used). `compute_yield_on_cost_dependency_identity()` feeds the report cache key.

**Frontend**: `frontend/src/lib/components/dashboard/YieldOnCostCell.svelte` inside `ExposureTable.svelte`.

## Dependencies
- [[F-054]] Dashboard — the report that carries the holdings.
- [[F-056]] FIFO at runtime — the D-1 eligible quantity comes from the FIFO lot engine.
- [[F-097]] WAC — the denominator is the residual average cost in the report currency.

## Implementation notes
The definition, its status contract and the five alternatives it rejected: [[decisions/yield-on-cost-definition]].
Income comes from the user's transactions, never from `AssetEvent`; TAX and FEE are excluded (gross yield).
