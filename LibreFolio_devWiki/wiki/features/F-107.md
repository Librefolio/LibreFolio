# F-107 — PAC allocator

## What it does
The first Tool of [[features/F-106]] answers one question: with the money available for this contribution cycle,
which purchases bring the allocation as close as possible to its target? It is a «pure PAC»: the calculation starts
from an empty portfolio, takes the cash and contributions the user selects, and plans transfers between brokers,
currency conversions and buy orders in each broker's increments — whole units, or amounts for fractional buying. A
guided planner of nine steps (Scenario, Liquidity, Brokers, Assets, Routing, Targets, FX only when needed, Strategy,
Review) builds the scenario; the result shows the plan per broker, the ledger of every cash pool, the exposures
before and after, and the proof of what was established. Nothing is bought and no order leaves LibreFolio.

Domain: [[domains/calculations]].

## Layer breakdown
**Backend**: the engine `backend/app/services/pac_allocator/` — `normalize.py` (validated request → exact
scenario, structured issues), `numeric.py` (`ExactRatio`, posting helpers), `ledger.py` (one posting per cash
movement, `_PLAN_ROUNDING`), `evaluator.py` (`evaluate_exact_candidate`, the exact replay), `compiler.py`,
`constraints.py` and `objectives.py` (the SCIP model), `solver.py` (the lexicographic cascade on PySCIPOpt),
`proof.py`, `planner.py` (`plan_pac_allocation`), `planner_report.py` and `wire_numbers.py` (the published result),
`issues.py`, `models.py`. The plugin `backend/app/services/tool_plugins/pac_allocator/plugin.py` (`PacAllocatorTool`, service
`pac_allocator`, operation `plan`, contract and implementation `1.0.0`) claims a 30 s engine window and keeps 2 s
for the report. The contract is `backend/app/schemas/pac_allocator.py` (`PacPlannerRequest`); the copies come from
`POST /api/v1/portfolio/allocation-source` (`backend/app/services/portfolio_allocation_source.py`). The solver is
PySCIPOpt `6.2.1` (SCIP 10), pinned in the `Pipfile` by the developer (`941834237`).

**Frontend**: `frontend/src/lib/features/tools/pac-allocator/planner/` — `PacPlannerTool.svelte`, `steps/` (one
component per step), `result/` (outcome, KPI cards, operational plan, ledger, exposures, proof), the draft
(`draft.svelte.ts`), the request builder (`request.ts`), the copies (`source.ts`, `copies.ts`, `sourceLoad.svelte.ts`)
and exact decimal formatting (`decimal.ts`, `format.ts`). The draft lives only in the open page.

## Implementation notes
- **How a plan is found and trusted**: [[decisions/pac-scip-searches-exact-replay-certifies]]. **What «closest»
  means**: [[decisions/pac-lexicographic-fixed-l2-objective]]. **Currencies**:
  [[decisions/pac-fx-one-rate-map-direct-conversions]] and [[decisions/pac-rounding-against-the-plan]].
  **Data from the portfolio**: [[decisions/pac-draft-copies-from-owned-data]]. **The wire**:
  [[decisions/pac-contract-compacted-before-release]].
- **Seven outcomes** (`result_state`): `needs_input`, `invalid`, `unsupported`, and four «ready» states —
  `ready_no_op`, `ready_incumbent`, `ready_infeasible`, `ready_no_incumbent`. A plan stopped by a limit, or whose exact
  check does not match the solver's own numbers, is published as the best found and marked **Optimality not proven**.
- **Capacity, measured on 2026-10-05**: with two brokers offering the same assets at different fees the solver
  proves the optimum up to 12 assets, with three brokers up to 8; a coarse grid is already hard at 5 × 2. How many
  assets to support is question (b) of the Rebalancer analysis.
- **Only `proportional`** is on the wire (Q-C0-4, 2026-09-24: «semplicemente non mettiamola, la ui in questo deve
  essere dinamica»); `min_fragmentation` and the margin variant wait for their SCIP cascade.

## Dead ends / Ideas explored
- **P1 `analyze`**, an analysis-only pilot with a Rebalancer card beside it — removed on 2026-09-21
  ([[decisions/pac-p1-prototype-removed]]).
- **Three UI rounds rejected** (2, 3, 4, 2026-09-11 → 14): the developer's review found the fourth, a working
  two-service prototype, «theoretically descriptive rather than operationally useful» (decision chronicle), and the
  operational redesign followed — see [[sources/phase00-pac-rebalancer-design-2026-09]].
- **The exhaustive oracle in production** — D's own reading of the brief; it exhausted the time budget on ordinary
  plans and became test-only (D-X1).
- **A mandatory route cap** with a high default (Q-C0-5) — the cap became optional (`NoOrderCap`, 2026-09-30).
- **FX extras** — a per-broker spread, a safety margin, a conversion fee, multi-hop chains: «❌ non più da fare»
  (developer, 2026-10-02).

- the Asset column cuts «MSFT Microsoft Corporation» without «…» while the marquee rests (`MarqueeName.svelte`);
- on a phone the Actions column of the Review and Targets tables misbehaves —
  [[problems/datatable-actions-column-on-mobile]];
- option (e): one conversion per broker and currency pair (splitting no longer pays, but is not merged);
- dedicated PAC E2E tests, written together with the Rebalancer's (developer, 2026-10-05); a backend engine E2E first;
- no developer page on the engine; `fx_cost` is always zero.
- *Plan 53, T-PAC (2026-10-13)*: the analysis of the PAC's tests is closed and the developer decided «Tutto dopo la
  1.2» — every test lot waits until after the release, no code now. Four defects found on the way, all after 1.2:
  C-73 (D1, the planner's percentages round ties against its own rule R14), C-74 (D2, the help of «Value after the
  plan» is wrong for the PAC), C-75 (D3, a SCIP bound of −0.0 would fail the result's validation), C-76 (D4, the
  planner's local gate checks neither sign nor zero). D1 is not the prudent rounding: the margin lives in the plan's
  computation — postings rounded against the plan, credits down and debits up, and the execution margin —
  never in the display ([[decisions/pac-rounding-against-the-plan]]).
