# F-108 — Portfolio Rebalancer

## What it does
Planned. The second Tool of the PAC family brings a portfolio **already invested** toward its targets: unlike the
PAC allocator ([[features/F-107]]), which starts from an empty portfolio, the Rebalancer counts the current
positions (`V0 > 0`), and may sell when the user authorises it — with a tax reserve on the realised gains and the
average cost of each position.

## What exists today
- **The design**: the five authoritative designs of `13_pacAllocator/` (2026-09-16) cover it next to the PAC — the
  policies `invest_only` (buy only, with turnover as a later criterion) and `invest_and_sell` (funding-only sales, each
  irreducible by one quantum, solved after the best `invest_only` plan is frozen), the SELL verifier, the tax reserve
  on the positive gain against the average cost (an Asset input `capital_gains_tax_rate` that the UI pre-fills at
  26 %, from the developer's correction of 2026-09-15), the margin variant shared with the PAC.
- **The contract, without a service**: `backend/app/schemas/pac_allocator.py` declares the Rebalancer requests
  (`RebalancerInvestOnlyRequest`, `RebalancerInvestAndSellRequest`), its issue codes and `SellIrreducibilityCheck`;
  `normalize_rebalancer_plan()` exists and is reached only by tests; a fixture pair
  (`rebalancer_plan_request.medium.v2.json`) pins the wire. No plugin publishes a `portfolio_rebalancer` service.
- **A withdrawn prototype**: the P1 pilot showed a Portfolio Rebalancer card next to the PAC; both were removed on
  2026-09-21 ([[decisions/pac-p1-prototype-removed]]). The user page of the Tools says so, so that an old bookmark
  is explained.

## Dependencies
- [[F-107]] PAC allocator — the same normalizer, exact ledger, evaluator, SCIP cascade, report and planner UI, to be
  reused as much as possible.
- [[F-106]] Tool platform — a second service on the same platform, with its own compiled renderer.

## Open questions (README row 14, «Analisi del Rebalancer — piano da definire»)
- **Question (b)**: how many assets the engine must handle, with measurements (the PAC proved its optimum up to 12
  assets with two brokers and up to 8 with three on 2026-10-05).
- **One contract version jump** for the whole Rebalancer, not one per slice.
- The order of the slices proposed on 2026-10-02: first `invest_only`, with fixtures and the oracle, then SCIP; then
  `invest_and_sell` with the SELL verifier — «L'ordine lo decidono developer e coordinator».
- `SellIrreducibilityCheck.closure_kind` still names `exhaustive_oracle`, a proof form D-X1 removed from production.
- The PAC's dedicated E2E tests are written together with the Rebalancer's (developer, 2026-10-05).

The developer asked on 2026-10-02: «Quando faremo il ribilanciamento?»; workstream D answered that it is «la
prossima fetta dopo l'integrazione». The folder `13_pacAllocator/` stays active for it (developer, 2026-10-10).
