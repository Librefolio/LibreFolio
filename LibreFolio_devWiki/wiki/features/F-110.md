# F-110 — Risk engine

## What it does
The backend half of Risk Analysis: one request to `POST /api/v1/risk/query` names a scope — an asset, an asset set,
or the portfolio with an optional subset of brokers (`broker_ids`) — and the analytics to run; the engine prepares the return series on a common market calendar,
checks eligibility and budgets, runs each analytic plugin and answers with results, warnings and data-quality issues.
`GET /risk/catalog` lists the analytics, `GET /risk/scenario-catalog` the stress scenarios, `POST /risk/eligibility`
says what a selection can run.

## Layer breakdown
**Backend**: `services/risk/service.py` (the plan of analytics, eligibility, budgets; split by theme on 2026-10-16, CP-9a: `scope.py`, `replay.py`, `results.py`, `data_quality.py`, `plan.py`), `metrics.py` (the formulas: VaR
and CVaR, the return histogram, the risk-free rate per period), `base.py` (the plugin base), `quant/` (QuantLib and
riskfolio-lib in `spawn` processes), `scenario_catalog/` (YAML scenarios); `market_series.py` and
`market_calendar.py`; fifteen plugins under `risk_plugins/`, one folder each since U-1, each with its own
`algorithm_version`; the contracts in `schemas/risk.py` and `api/v1/risk.py`.

## Dependencies
- Price histories and FX rates (the series), and the portfolio engine for portfolio scopes.

## Enables
- [[F-111]] the Risk tab of the Dashboard and broker pages, [[F-112]] the Lab of Asset Global, [[F-113]] the Risk tab
  of Asset Detail.

## Implementation notes
- The quant libraries run only in spawned worker processes: [[decisions/risk-quant-engine-process-boundary]].
- Returns come from prices alone (total return with coupons and dividends is open, A5).
- The histogram's grid is pinned on the VaR cut ([[decisions/histogram-grid-pinned-to-var-cut]]); the annualisation
  factor is observed from each series ([[problems/annualization-factor-not-coverage]]); the shared shapes between the
  first-round streams are the contracts of [[concepts/risk-contracts-k1-k9]]; which maths is ours and which the library's:
  [[decisions/risk-library-versus-own-maths]]; the September verdicts per tool: [[decisions/risk-tool-verdicts]].
- A replay excludes an asset not priced at both ends of the crisis instead of moving the joint baseline:
  [[problems/replay-baseline-shortened-by-late-starter]].
- Built in two rounds of workstream «Risk» in Phase 0, folder 02 — [[sources/phase00-risk-integration-2026-09]]; the
  archived backend-first plan G0–G6 is [[sources/phase00-risk-analysis-backend]].
