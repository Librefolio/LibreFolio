# F-111 — Risk tab (Dashboard and broker pages)

## What it does
A tab on the Dashboard, and the same panel on each broker page, that answers four questions in order:
**L1** «How much can it hurt?» (losses, VaR, drawdown), **L2** «Am I as diversified as I think?», **L3** «Am I being
paid for this risk?» (one table with volatility, return, Sortino and Sharpe, beta and correlation with a benchmark), and
**L4** «What if…?» (stress scenarios, then a simulation). Each level speaks for itself: notices and missing data show
where they apply.

## Layer breakdown
**Frontend**: `features/risk/levels/` — `RiskLevelsPanel.svelte` (mounted by the Dashboard and by the broker page),
`RiskLevelSection.svelte`, `l1/`, `L2Diversification.svelte`, `L3RiskAdjusted.svelte`, `L3Benchmark.svelte`,
`L4WhatIf.svelte` and `l4/`; state in `features/risk/` (`riskStore.svelte.ts`, `riskPanelController.svelte.ts`,
`riskBenchmarkStore.svelte.ts`). **Backend**: [[F-110]].
