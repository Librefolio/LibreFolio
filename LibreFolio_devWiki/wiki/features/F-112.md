# F-112 — Risk Lab (Asset Global)

## What it does
On the Assets page, the Correlation tab is a laboratory for a selection of assets: build the selection, then read the
correlation heatmap, «How much did each of these hurt?», «What did each of these pay for its risk?» (a risk–return
chart against a chosen benchmark) and «What if…?» (a replay). Ineligible assets are parked, not dropped.

## Layer breakdown
**Frontend**: `features/risk/AssetSetRiskPanel.svelte` (mounted by the Assets page), `AssetSetComparisonLevels.svelte`,
`RiskReturnLevel.svelte` and `riskReturnLevel.ts`, `AssetSetCorrelationSection.svelte`, `AssetSetReplaySection.svelte`,
`LabAssetPicker.svelte`, `assetSetSelection.ts`, `eligibility.ts`; shared `BenchmarkSelect.svelte`,
`CorrelationHeatmap.svelte`, `charts/ScatterChart.svelte`. **Backend**: [[F-110]] (asset-set scope).
