# F-113 — Risk tab (Asset Detail)

## What it does
The Risk tab of an asset's page shows risk figures and scenarios for that one asset. It has not been rebuilt on the
four levels: it keeps its beta notice in 1.2 («Asset Detail keeps its beta notice: its risk view has not been rebuilt
yet», CHANGELOG).

## Layer breakdown
**Frontend**: `components/risk/AssetRiskScenariosView.svelte` mounts the monolithic `RiskAnalysisPanel.svelte`, with
tracking error, information ratio and `sobol_start_index`. **Backend**: [[F-110]] (asset scope).
