# F-113 — Risk tab (Asset Detail)

## What it does
The **Risk & Scenarios** tab of an asset's page is an x-ray of that one asset: the Dashboard's levels panel
([[F-111]]) on scope `asset`, so it keeps only the levels that have a meaning on one asset — L1 «How much can it
hurt?», L3 «Compared with a reference» (the shared benchmark, never the asset itself; no tracking error nor
information ratio), L4 «What if…?» (a replay in one sentence, a shock by sector or region, never by asset type; a
simulation). No L2: one asset has nothing to diversify. Since RN-17 (1.4, 17/11/2026); before it, a legacy beta panel.

## Layer breakdown
**Frontend**: `features/risk/AssetRiskTab.svelte` reads once the value of your position today (`POST /portfolio/report`
grouped by asset, `assetRiskTabRules.ts`), says once which value the amounts are on, notes when prices come from a plan
(scheduled investment, HOLD, crowdfunding), and mounts `levels/RiskLevelsPanel.svelte` on the asset. Money on one asset
goes through `riskAnalysisHelpers.amountOnValue`, the one place a risk level turns a fraction into money. The panel
draws no sync button of its own there (the page's toolbar holds Sync and Reload, Y11); the sync's completion
(`{accepted}`) reaches the page unchanged. **Backend**: [[F-110]]; `asset_risk_return` answers scope `asset` too
(weight 1: the pair is the asset's own, read by L3 and by RN-18).
