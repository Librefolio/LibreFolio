# F-114 — Benchmark assets

## What it does
An asset can be marked as a benchmark with a **Benchmark** switch in its edit dialog; the assets so marked are offered
as comparison benchmarks, as in the Correlation workbench of the Assets page ([[F-112]]). Registered on 2026-10-13,
when the feature pages were checked against the verified 1.2.0 CHANGELOG: it had no page of its own.

## Layer breakdown
**Backend**: `is_benchmark` on the asset (`backend/app/db/models.py`), in the asset schemas, with a tri-state filter
on the asset list (`backend/app/schemas/assets/assets.py`).

**Frontend**: the switch in `AssetModal.svelte`, carried by `assetEditData.ts`.

## Dependencies
- [[F-024]] — the asset it flags.
