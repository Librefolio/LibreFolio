# 🧠 Asset AI Export

Export one asset, with its market history and, if you hold it, your position, to ask an AI about
the asset or about your position in it. The options and how to paste are in the
[AI Export overview](index.md).

---

## 📍 Where to Find It

On an asset's detail page, select **AI Export** in the page toolbar. It first opens on
**Asset Market Analysis**.

The export covers the asset open on the page, in the currency the page shows, with the last day
of the page's date range as the export date. Your position counts every broker you can open.

---

## 📤 Export Data

| Choice | What you get |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Position & Market History (full)** | Your holdings in the asset (cost, value, P&L, performance, FIFO lots), a compact market context, and drawdown |
| **Market History Only (no holdings)** | Prices, returns, indicators, states, events and drawdown, without your holdings |

---

## 🎯 Analyses

| Analysis | What the AI does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Position Review** | Reviews your position's value, cost, P&L, brokers and lots, with market context; it needs a position in the asset |
| **Asset Market Analysis** | Analyzes price history, returns, trend, momentum, volatility, drawdown and events |
