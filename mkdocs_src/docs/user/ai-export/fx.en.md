# 🧠 FX AI Export

Export one currency pair, with its rates, its trend and your direct exposure to it, to ask an AI
about the pair or about what it means for what you hold. The options and how to paste are in the
[AI Export overview](index.md).

---

## 📍 Where to Find It

On a pair's detail page, select **AI Export** in the page toolbar. It first opens on
**FX Pair Analysis**.

The export covers the pair open on the page, with the last day of the page's date range as the
export date. Your exposure counts every broker you can open.

If LibreFolio holds rates for only part of the period you choose, the export uses those and says
which dates it covers: it never uses future rates.

---

## 📤 Export Data

| Choice | What you get |
| :--- | :--- |
| **FX Market & Exposure** | The current rate, a compact market and timing context, and your direct exposure to the pair |
| **FX Market History** | Detailed rates, returns, volatility, indicators, states and events, with coverage |

---

## 🎯 Analyses

| Analysis | What the AI does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **FX Pair Analysis** | Analyzes the pair's direction, returns, trend, momentum, volatility and events, with dated macro context |
| **FX Exposure Impact** | Describes how your cash and positions are directly linked to the pair, without looking inside funds; it needs at least one such link |
