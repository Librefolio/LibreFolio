# 🗂️ Classification

The **Profile** tab of the asset page opens with the asset's **sector** and **geographic** distribution, when this data is in the asset's profile (set by hand or read by its enrichers). Below it come the description, the key figures and the details.

## 📊 Sector Distribution

A **pie chart** showing the breakdown by industry sector (e.g., Technology 35%, Healthcare 20%, Finance 15%...). This is especially useful for ETFs and diversified funds to understand the underlying sector allocation.

## 🌍 Geographic Distribution

### World Map

An **interactive world map** with countries colored by allocation weight. Hover over a country to see its percentage.

### Country Breakdown

A **pie chart** showing the percentage breakdown by country or region (e.g., United States 60%, Europe 25%, Asia 15%...).

---

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. Open the **Profile** tab, between **Overview** and **Risk & Scenarios**
2. The classification is at the top, with the date of its data («data as of …», or «from …» when the source gives none)
3. When the composition changed in the period chosen at the top of the page, pick another date to see that day's
4. Hover over pie chart slices or map regions for details

---

## ⚙️ Configuring Classification

Classification data is part of the asset's profile, and can be set in two ways:

1. **From its sources**: the asset's enrichers (justETF, Yahoo Finance, Borsa Italiana…) read the sector and geographic data and fill the profile
2. **By hand**: **Edit data** in the **Profile** tab, or the **Profile data** row of the asset form (✏️), opens the **Profile data** window, where each distribution has its own editor
