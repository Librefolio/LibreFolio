# ⚙️ Settings

LibreFolio's Settings panel lets you personalise the application and, if you are an administrator, manage global configuration.

Everyone has four tabs and the **Administration** group, which gathers the tabs of the instance; only administrators see **Users**, **Backup**, **Email** and **Security** in it:

- 👤 **[Profile](profile.md)** — Username, email, avatar, password, and account deletion.
- 🎛️ **[Preferences](preferences.md)** — Language, base currency, and theme.
- 🧩 **[Plugins](plugins.md)** — What the price and exchange-rate sources ask for: your own search preferences, and the instance's settings, which only administrators change.
- ℹ️ **[About](about.md)** — License info, version numbers, installed plugins, and the in-app changelog.
- 🗂️ **Administration** — The tabs of the instance, on the right of the bar on a wide screen:
    - ⚙️ **[Global Settings](../../admin/settings.md)** — Instance-wide configuration, visible to everyone but editable only by superusers.
    - 👥 **[Users](../../admin/users.md)** — The accounts of the instance, invitations and password-reset links (administrators only).
    - 💾 **[Backup](../../admin/cli_tools.md#backup)** — The data of the whole instance in one archive, to download (administrators only).
    - ✉️ **[Email](../../admin/email.md#email-tab)** — Whether LibreFolio can send emails, and a test email (administrators only).
    - 🛡️ **Security** — How the instance is configured, with what needs attention first (administrators only): see «Security» in [Global Settings](../../admin/settings.md).

---

## 🗂️ The Administration Group {: #administration }

Click **Administration** to open its first tab, **Global Settings**. For an administrator, a second row of tabs then shows under the first one: **Global Settings**, **Users**, **Backup**, **Email** and **Security**. Clicking **Administration** again while one of them is open keeps you on it.

- 👤 **Not an administrator?** Your **Administration** holds **Global Settings** alone, read-only: it opens straight away, without a second row.
- 🔢 **A number on Security**, repeated on **Administration**, counts what the security checks of the instance ask an administrator to look at.
- 📱 **On a phone**, the tabs show their icons only, and the title above them names the open tab.

## ↔️ When the Tabs Do Not Fit {: #tabs-overflow }

In a narrow window, or in a language with longer names such as French or Spanish, a row of tabs can be wider than the panel. The row then scrolls sideways, and fades out on each side where more tabs are hidden. When you open a tab, resize the window or change the language, the row scrolls so that the open tab shows whole.
